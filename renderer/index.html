SiteSlurp

Paste a link. Slurp the whole site.

Rendered in a real browser, then unpacked into clean editable files you own.

Follows the links it finds and saves every page it can reach. Turn this off to grab only the single page you pasted.

How many clicks away from the starting page it is allowed to travel. Depth 1 is that page plus everything linked from it; depth 2 also follows the links on those pages, and so on. Each step multiplies the page count, so 4–5 on a big site can mean thousands of pages and a very long run.

Pretends to be a phone, so responsive sites hand over their mobile design instead of the desktop one.

The capture engine drops video files to keep snapshots small. This puts the original video URLs back so the pages still play.

Removes consent popups and the overlays that come with them, so the saved pages open clean.

Runs the capture in a visible window instead of in the background. Useful for sites that block bots, or when you need to log in or dismiss something by hand.

Pauses after the page loads so slow animations, lazy images and late-loading content finish before the snapshot is taken.

What to call this capture on disk. Leave it empty and the domain is used.
